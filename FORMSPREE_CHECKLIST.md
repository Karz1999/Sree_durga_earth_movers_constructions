# Formspree Registration Checklist

- [ ] Go to https://formspree.io/
- [ ] Sign up or log in to your Formspree account
- [ ] Confirm your account email address
- [ ] Create a new form project
- [ ] Copy the generated form endpoint URL
- [ ] Replace `action="https://formspree.io/f/mayvlqyo"` in `index.html` with your endpoint
- [ ] Optionally add any required hidden inputs for subject, redirect, or form settings
- [ ] Save the updated `index.html`
- [ ] Publish the site to GitHub Pages
- [ ] Test the enquiry form by submitting a sample message
- [ ] Verify the email is received in your Formspree inbox or configured recipient
